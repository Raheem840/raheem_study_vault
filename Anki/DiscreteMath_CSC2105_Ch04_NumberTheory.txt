State the Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic.	Every integer greater than 1 can be written as a product of primes in exactly one way, up to ordering
Describe the Euclidean Algorithm for computing gcd(a,b).	Repeatedly replace the larger number with the remainder of dividing it by the smaller, until the remainder is 0; the last nonzero remainder is the gcd
State the identity relating gcd and lcm.	gcd(a,b) * lcm(a,b) = a * b
What does the Extended Euclidean Algorithm find, beyond the gcd?	Integers x, y such that ax + by = gcd(a,b) (Bezout's identity), used for computing modular inverses
Define a ≡ b (mod m).	m divides (a - b); a and b leave the same remainder when divided by m
Under what condition does ax ≡ b (mod m) have a solution?	If and only if gcd(a,m) divides b
When does a have a modular inverse mod m, and how is it found?	When gcd(a,m) = 1 (a and m are coprime); found via the Extended Euclidean Algorithm
In RSA, what are the public key components and what is the encryption formula?	Public key is (n, e); encryption is C = M^e mod n
In RSA, what is the private key and the decryption formula?	Private key is d (where d = e^-1 mod phi(n)); decryption is M = C^d mod n
Why is RSA considered secure?	Computing the private key d from the public key alone requires factoring n back into its prime factors p and q, which is believed computationally infeasible for sufficiently large primes, even though multiplying p*q is easy
What is Euler's totient phi(n) for n = p*q where p, q are distinct primes?	phi(n) = (p-1)(q-1)
