## How the chapters connect (cross-links, not just a flat list)
- Ch.12 Boolean Algebra is literally Ch.1 Logic relabeled for hardware — read them back to back.
- Ch.8 Recurrence Relations explains WHERE Ch.3's O(n log n) complexity class mathematically comes from (merge sort's recurrence).
- Ch.5 Induction is the proof technique used to verify Ch.8's closed-form solutions and Ch.3's recursive algorithm correctness.
- Ch.9 Relations underpins the relational database model — directly relevant to your Databases (CSC2107) course once we build that vault.
